Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

LinAI Systems ("we," "us," or "our") operates the LinAI Systems platform, including the Lintone AI phone assistant service, online ordering websites, and management dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Phone Numbers: We collect your phone number when you call a restaurant using our AI phone assistant, create an account on a restaurant's online ordering website, or place an order by phone.
  • Order Information: We collect details about your food orders, including items ordered, delivery addresses, order totals, and payment status.
  • Delivery Addresses: When you place a delivery order, we collect and validate your address using Google Maps to calculate delivery distance and fees.
  • Call Data: When you interact with our AI phone assistant, we process call audio in real time to understand and fulfill your order. We do not store call recordings.
  • SMS Communication: We send transactional SMS messages including verification codes, online ordering links, and payment links. We collect your phone number and SMS opt-in/opt-out status.
  • Account Data: If you create an account, we store your phone number, saved addresses, and order history associated with specific restaurants.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your food orders
  • Send transactional SMS messages (verification codes, order links, payment links)
  • Verify your identity through SMS verification codes
  • Validate delivery addresses and calculate delivery fees
  • Improve our AI phone assistant's accuracy and performance
  • Communicate with you about your orders
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

3. SMS Messaging

By providing your phone number through our services, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages from us. These messages are limited to:

  • Login verification codes
  • Online ordering website links (when phone ordering encounters issues)
  • Payment links for completing orders

We do not send marketing or promotional SMS messages. Message frequency varies based on your usage. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchase.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

  • Telnyx: Telephony and SMS messaging services
  • Stripe: Payment processing for online orders
  • Google Maps: Address validation and delivery distance calculation
  • Google Cloud (Firestore): Secure data storage
  • Google Gemini: AI-powered conversation processing
  • Deepgram: Speech-to-text transcription for phone calls

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing the use of your information. We encourage you to review their policies.

5. Data Retention

  • SMS verification codes expire and are deleted after 10 minutes.
  • Order data is retained for business and legal purposes.
  • Customer accounts (phone number, addresses) are retained until you request deletion.
  • Call audio is processed in real time and is not stored.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure webhook signature verification
  • Rate limiting on authentication endpoints
  • Access controls on all administrative systems

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP at any time.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@linaisystems.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
