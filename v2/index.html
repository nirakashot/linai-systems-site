Why this matters

The phone is still part of service.

When the kitchen gets busy, taking a phone order competes with the work already in front of your team. LinAI is designed to keep that order in a clear, confirmable path to the workflow your restaurant already knows.

How it works

One number. Three clear steps.

01

Your customer calls as usual.

Your restaurant number remains the number customers know.

02

LinAI works through the menu.

It captures items and quantities, then confirms the order with the caller.

03

The ticket joins your workflow.

The confirmed order is sent into your existing thermal-printer workflow.

Questions, clearly answered

The practical details.

Do customers call a new number?

No. LinAI is designed to answer your existing restaurant number, so customers continue to call the number they already know.

How does the kitchen get the order?

Confirmed orders are sent into the restaurant’s existing thermal-printer workflow.

What does a phone order cost?

AI phone orders are billed at $1.59 per completed phone order, with no marketplace commission.

Can we also take orders online?

Yes. Direct online ordering is available as an optional add-on at $0.99 per completed web order.
