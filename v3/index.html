Practical details

Questions from
the counter.

Do customers call a new number?

No. LinAI is designed to answer your existing restaurant number, so customers keep calling the number they already know.

How does the kitchen get the order?

Confirmed orders are sent into the restaurant’s thermal-printer workflow. Compatibility depends on your setup; we review that workflow during pilot planning.

What does a completed phone order cost?

LinAI phone orders are billed at $1.59 per completed phone order. This is an order-handling fee, not a percentage marketplace commission.

Can we also take orders online?

Direct online ordering is an optional add-on at $0.99 per completed web order.

What about lunch specials and combo choices?

The ordering experience is built around the restaurant’s own menu structure, so choices such as rice, included sides, and combo options can stay connected to the correct entrée. We review your actual menu during setup.
